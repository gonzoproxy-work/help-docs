# Which Websites Don't Work Through Our Proxies

Most websites open through our proxies without any problems. The exception is financial websites: they are closed by default. Below we explain how this works and what to do if you still need such a site.

### Financial Websites

If a financial website doesn't open while all the others work normally, the cause is not your GEO settings and not the proxy itself. We close such resources in advance, and the restriction can only be lifted after KYC, that is, confirmation of your identity.

### How to Request Access to a Site

#### Steps

1\. Complete KYC.

Verification is done in your personal account, in the settings section.

2\. Contact support through the bot.

Submit a request in[ @GonzoProxy\_bot](https://t.me/GonzoProxy_bot) and name the specific site you need.

3\. Describe your task.

Tell us why you need this site and how you plan to use it. The more detail you give, the easier it is to make a decision.

4\. Wait for the decision.

Our team will review the request and let you know what it decided.

#### Why the Decision Isn't Automatic

Financial websites require special care. Our proxies run on the devices of real people, and we make sure nothing happens through them that could harm the device owners, you, or the service. That is why our team reviews each request separately, and a positive answer is not guaranteed.
