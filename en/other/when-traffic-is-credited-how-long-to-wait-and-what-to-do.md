# When Traffic Is Credited: How Long to Wait and What to Do

Traffic doesn't appear in your account instantly. First the transfer goes through the network, then the payment provider processes the payment, and only after that does the traffic land in your account. This usually takes a few minutes, and you don't need to do anything.

### How Long to Wait

Give the payment up to 20 minutes after the network confirms the transfer for the first time. Count from that moment, not from the moment you sent it. You can check the confirmation status in any blockchain explorer using the transaction hash.

If the payment sits in processing at the payment provider for a while, there is no need to worry. Your money hasn't gone anywhere: the transfer is already on the network, and the provider just needs time to process it.

### If More Than 20 Minutes Have Passed

Then write to our support at[ @GonzoProxy\_bot](https://t.me/GonzoProxy_bot). Tell us which currency and network you paid with (for example, USDT on TRC20), the amount, and when you sent the transfer. And be sure to send the transaction hash. With it we can quickly find the payment and see at which stage it stopped, so there will be almost no clarifying questions.

It is better to send the hash as text rather than as a screenshot, as it is easier to check.

### Where to Find the Transaction Hash

The hash, also known as the TXID, is in the transfer details. Open the transaction history in the wallet or exchange you paid from, find the transfer, and copy the whole hash. If you can't find it, we have a detailed guide:[ what a transaction hash is and where to find it](https://help.gonzoproxy.com/ru/kak-naiti-hash-tranzakcii-i-kakie-byvayut-seti-kriptovalyut).

### What Not to Do

Don't send a repeat payment until you know what happened with the first one. You could accidentally pay twice. And don't delete the hash until the traffic appears in your account, as you may need it.
