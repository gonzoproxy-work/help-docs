# How to Request a Refund

If a payment was made by mistake, or the service didn't meet your expectations right after purchase, write to us: support at[ @GonzoProxy\_bot](https://t.me/GonzoProxy_bot) or by email at help@gonzoproxy.com. If the refund request is approved, the refund is processed within 14 days.

The conditions under which a refund is possible are described in the[ refund policy](https://gonzoproxy.com/return-policy).

### What to Send

So that your request can be reviewed right away, without clarifying questions, include in your message:

1. Account email. The email your account is registered with. We use it to find your account and payment.
2. TRC-20 wallet address. The wallet the funds should be returned to. It must be an address on the TRC-20 network (USDT on TRON).
3. Reason for the refund. A couple of sentences: what happened and why you need a refund. It helps us understand the situation and improve the service.

### Check Your Wallet Address

Crypto transfers can't be reversed. If you specify an address from a different network or with a typo, the funds may not arrive. Before sending, check that:

* the address is copied in full, not typed manually;
* the wallet accepts TRC-20 specifically;
* the address belongs to you, not to an exchange that doesn't support such transfers directly.

### Timing

* We process refunds within 14 days.
* The reply will come to the same chat or email you wrote from.
* If we need clarifications, we will write to you there. The more complete your request, the faster the decision.

### Before You Submit a Request

If the reason is speed or a proxy that stopped working, try creating a new one. With us, this is the standard way to switch the device and IP, and in most cases it solves the problem. If it doesn't help, write to us and we will look into your case
