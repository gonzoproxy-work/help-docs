# Is There a Free Trial?

We don't have a free trial period. What we do have is the promo code START15: it gives a 15% discount, and with it you can test the proxies on your own task with minimal investment.

### Why Don't We Offer a Free Trial?

Our proxies run on real people's devices that rent out their IP. Every connection belongs to a live owner, so free access would mean giving away other people's resources. It is more honest to offer a low-cost start with a discount than to promise a trial that doesn't exist.

### How to Test the Proxies at a Minimal Cost

1. Apply the promo code START15 at checkout. The discount will be 15%.
2. Start with a small volume. Check how the proxy behaves on your actual task, not on an abstract speed test.
3. Set up GEO from broad to narrow: country, state, then city or provider, only if you need them. This way there are more matching devices and the result is more stable.
4. If the speed or stability didn't meet your needs, create a new proxy. With us, this is the standard way to switch the device, and with it the IP, the plan, and the channel load. Speed variation between proxies is normal for a network of real devices, and the first connection doesn't show the whole service.
