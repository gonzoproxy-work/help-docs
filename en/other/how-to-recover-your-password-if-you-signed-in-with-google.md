# How to Recover Your Password If You Signed In with Google

If you registered or signed in with Google, you may not have a separate password for our service, since Google confirms your sign-in. So when you need to sign in another way, or you simply forgot your password, you need to recover it by email. It is quick and doesn't require contacting support.

### How to Recover Your Password

1. Sign out of your account if you are currently signed in.
2. On the sign-in page, click "Forgot password."
3. Enter the email your account is registered with. It must be the same email you used when signing in with Google.
4. Open the email and copy the temporary password. It will be sent to the email you entered. If the email isn't in your inbox, check the "Spam" folder.
5. Sign in with the temporary password.
6. Set a permanent password. In your personal account, open the "Security" section and change the password in the settings.

The temporary password is only for signing in. Don't keep it as your main password: replace it with your own right after signing in.

### If Something Doesn't Work

* The email doesn't arrive. Check that the email address was entered without typos, look in the "Spam" folder, wait a few minutes, and then request the password again.
* You're not sure which email is linked. Try the one used in your Google account.
* Nothing helped. Write to support:[ @GonzoProxy\_bot](https://t.me/GonzoProxy_bot).
