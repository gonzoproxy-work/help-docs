# How to Choose the Right GEO

Our proxies run on real devices, so the list of available options depends on which devices are online right now. Choose your parameters in order, from broad to narrow. For example:

1. Country. A required parameter; every selection starts with it.
2. State or region. Narrows the choice to the territory you need, but still leaves enough devices.
3. City or provider. These are the narrowest filters. Add them only if you really need them for your task.

Each next parameter cuts off some of the devices. The narrower your choice, the fewer matching devices there are, and the higher the chance that the one you need won't be online.

### Why Is a Provider Sometimes Unavailable?

Not all providers are available all the time. A proxy runs on a real person's device, and they may turn off their computer, close the app, or go offline. If no powered-on devices from the provider you need are left in the selected region or city, that provider is unavailable in the list, or a proxy with it works unstably.

This is not a service error. It happens with any rental of live devices: the set of available providers changes throughout the day.

### What to Do If the Provider You Need Is Unavailable

1. Choose a different provider. A neighboring operator in the same region is often just as good.
2. Remove the provider altogether. Keep the country, state, and, if needed, the city. Many more devices will match, and a proxy will be found right away.
3. Widen the filter. If that didn't help, replace the city with the whole state.
4. Try again later. Device owners turn their devices on at different times, and the provider's availability may come back.

If your proxy stopped working after you selected a provider, create a new one without it. This is the fastest way to check whether the provider was the cause.

### In Short

* Go from broad to narrow: country, state, then city or provider.
* Specify a provider only if it is critical for your task.
* If a provider is unavailable, change it or remove it. This is the standard solution.
