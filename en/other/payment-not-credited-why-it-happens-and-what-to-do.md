# Payment Not Credited: Why It Happens and What to Do

A crypto payment goes through several stages: you send the transfer, the network confirms it, the payment provider registers the receipt, and only then does the amount appear on your balance. Between the second and third stages, a payment sometimes spends a while in processing at the payment provider. Your funds are not lost: the transfer is already on the network, and the provider just needs time to process it.

That is why your balance may not change in the first few minutes after payment. This is normal.

Wait 20 minutes after the network's first confirmation of your transfer. Count from the moment the network confirmed the transaction, not from the moment you sent it. You can check the confirmation status in any blockchain explorer using the transaction hash.

In most cases, the payment is credited on its own within this time, and you don't need to contact support.

### If Your Balance Hasn't Been Topped Up

Write to support:[ @GonzoProxy\_bot](https://t.me/GonzoProxy_bot). In your message, include:

1. Payment method: the currency and network you used (for example, USDT on TRC20).
2. TXID (transaction hash): the unique identifier of your transfer. We use it to find your payment and see at which stage it got stuck.
3. The amount and the time you sent it.

Tell us that more than 20 minutes have passed since the first confirmation and the payment has not been credited. This way we can start checking right away, without extra clarifying questions.

### Where to Find the TXID

You can copy the TXID from the wallet or exchange you sent the payment from: it is in the transaction details in your transfer history. Step-by-step guide:[ What Is a Transaction Hash and Where to Find It](https://help.gonzoproxy.com/ru/kak-naiti-hash-tranzakcii-i-kakie-byvayut-seti-kriptovalyut).

### How to Get This Resolved Faster

* Send the TXID as text, not only as a screenshot, so it is easier to check.
* Don't send a second payment until you know the status of the first one, as you risk overpaying.
* Keep the hash until the amount appears on your balance.
